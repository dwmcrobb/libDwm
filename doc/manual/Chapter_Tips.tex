\section{Implement \texttt{iostream} support first}
Make your type meet the requirements of
\hyperlink{concept:HasStreamRead}{\texttt{HasStreamRead<T>}} and
\hyperlink{concept:HasStreamWrite}{\ttfamily HasStreamWrite<T>}
first, by adding these two public members to your class or struct:

\begin{verbatim}
  std::ostream & Write(std::ostream & os) const;
  std::istream & Read(std::istream & is);
\end{verbatim}

The advantanges to doing this before adding support for other source
and sink types:

\begin{itemize}
\item Easier to unit test packing (write) and unpacking (read)
  correctness and symmetry, since \texttt{std::stringstream} may be
  used as source and sink (no interaction with the filesystem or
  network stack).
\item Can later be used to shortcut support for sources and sinks
  other than \texttt{std::istream} and \texttt{std::ostream}.
\item Can be leveraged to reduce read/write system calls (and
  potential context switches).
\item Allows some resource tradeoffs when used to shortcut support
  for other sinks and sources.
\end{itemize}
